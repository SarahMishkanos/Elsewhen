ANOMALY

Twenty years ago, on the night of a storm, your little sister Mara went down to the cellar to hide, and was never found. Tonight you are standing in the same house, and the power has failed again.

The house is still playing the game you played every night: spot what has changed. Find it. Report it. Remember everything. And never look away from the thing that watches you.

The power is off when you start. Light switches glow faintly green near each doorway \u2014 or use the matches on the hall table if you\u2019d rather not find them yet. You don\u2019t have many.
